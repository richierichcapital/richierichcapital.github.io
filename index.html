GM Research Scripts

GM Research Scripts is a private tool that its owner runs on their own laptop to support their own research on published economics and finance papers. It is not offered to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for.

What it does

The tool connects to its owner's Google account and works only with files it creates itself in that account's Google Drive. It uses these files to:

  • keep dated backup copies of the owner's research database in Drive folders;
  • keep a Google Sheet that shows a read-only copy of that database, with separate tabs where the owner can type requests to the tool.

It uses Google's drive.file permission, which limits it to files the tool created or the owner chose to open with it. It cannot see the owner's other Drive files.
